title: Hidden blocks revealing themselves when they are ready
date: 2026-11-05

The past week or so I've been very caught up on a particular role and company. 

The process was going smoothly, the role felt like a dream role, it ticked more boxes than I could have dreamed of. I envisioned how well that role would fit into my life. The culture, the work, the excitement.

Then came the technical interview, which involves live coding in front of two people. 

The process of interviewing for software roles is quite typical. It's pretty elaborate with multiple rounds, testing all sorts of abilities from technical competencies to values based competencies to ensure you will make a good fit.

I've participated on both sides of the interview. I know how it feels to be the assessor and the one being assessed. I know what companies tend to look for. 

I know to communicate my assumptions and make sure to ask questions along the way. I know that I need to communicate my thought process out loud so that I am bringing those who are evaluating me along for the ride. 

The challenges these days tend to range from very abstract, theoretical problem solving to real world applications. This one in particular was more real-world which in theory I should have absolutely thrived in since it's aligned my daily work looks like. But. I. did. not.

I left the interview immediately feeling completely vulnerable and exposed. The interviewers were super pleasant, I had a very good feeling about the company going into it (and still do post process), and the challenge was nothing extraordinary. But I gently panicked my way through it, my body feeling as though it was gearing up to run away. And sadly, this is just how it is for me.

I talk to people, and they say it's all in the preparation. I practiced precisely the challenges that were offered. I spent a good part of the morning meditating and doing breath work to try and remain calm. Not to mention, I have 8 years of experience doing the exact thing I was tested on. So, what the heck kind of preparation do I need?

I heard a rejection from this company, which stings on it's own because I was very excited about the opportunity. But what stings more is that the feedback I received is things that I am more than capable of doing, I was just incapable of showing in the interview setting.

So I am now sitting with that, and it has opened a bit of a can of worms for me.

This is a pattern that doesn't just show up in interviews. It shows up in women's circles when I am asked to share my intentions for why I'm there. It shows up in my grown up gym class when we start playing a game that's unfamiliar or solo. It shows up when I think about my wedding day and all the attention that's going to be on me. It is always there lurking beneath, ready to heat me up from the inside any time I am asked to be in a spotlight where I am fully witnessed.

It's been a thing for me since I was a teenager. Before then, I loved to be in the spotlight. I'd put on performances for my parents whenever I could. I don't know exactly where it comes from, but it's highly uncomfortable, and it's limiting me. I've done my best to manage it by reducing the number of experiences I have that require this discomfort.

But my life and my inner callings are all guiding me towards experiences that do involve me being witnessed and seen in front of others. My dream in life has always been building a space on a beautiful property where I can curate gatherings and circles on specific topics, which undoubtedly involves putting myself in the spotlight. I am also signed up for yoga teacher training which certainly involves being seen. 

So, I am ready to let go of this limitation. I need to let go of this limitation. No more bending my life around it. I want to work through it, and overcome it.

What does that look like?

It looks like gently challenging myself to put myself out there more. It looks like learning what I need and what I can do in order to feel safe within myself in moments of being witnessed by others. It looks like trusting in my ability to move through this, instead of accepting that I will always be this way.

It's a lot of self-discovery, and small steps towards this unfolding. I have multiple outlets to practice: this blog, my instagram, and my yoga teacher training. 

A lot of the messages I've been receiving from life have been about devoting our lives to the highest good of all and so I have been doing my daily devotional work to my highest self, and to my connection to the Universe. It feels like this sudden insight is a result of my own decision to purify myself to be a channel for miracles. This has been a huge limiting pressure on me, and the fact that I'm now becoming aware of it means it's ready to go. 

I'm excited for who I become through this process. And how life unfolds when I move from a place of courage and self-trust.
