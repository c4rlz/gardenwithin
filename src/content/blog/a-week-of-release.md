title: A week of release
date: 2026-10-09
description: >+
  We are in Libra season, and I think the first thing that comes to mind for
  most with Libra is the scale. It is all about balance. 


  I welcome more balance into my life. 


  The chapters that precede this one have felt demanding and challenging, though
  filled with many moments of beauty and gratitude. 


  It's hard to express my sentiment in words because it is so full of contrast
  and contradictions. It has been challenging, yet it has also been expansive
  and full of adventure. I have accomplished life long goals. I have been to
  places that I couldn't have even dreamt of. I have found my way back onto a
  beautiful path. And, it has all taken a toll on my body.


  I suppose life is full of paradoxes. 


  This week, the theme is "release". I didn't ask it to, it just naturally is.
  And so I am listening and aligning.


  I've said goodbye to my previous company. I've said goodbye to an entire bin
  of clothes I've been keeping around "just incase". I've pruned my plants.
  Tomorrow, I have a ceremony to say goodbye to my grandpa. And as part of that,
  we are also saying goodbye to my grandma. 


  This week has been asking for me to slow down and feel. 


  I am processing loss, and creating space so that next week I can start
  cultivating something new.
